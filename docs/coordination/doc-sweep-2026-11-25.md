## Patterns behind the drift

Most findings come from five causes:

1. **A decision lands in one place and its echoes elsewhere stay.**
   - RS settled D4.
   - LK took lakes from G3.
   - IX replaced "level 1 default", far forms and metre LOD.
   - ST0 turned tabs into kinds.
   - Parking per-tier borders left four ARCHITECTURE passages describing it.
2. **The scale-1 rebase (`e3976ec`)** halved the default region (4 km → 2 km)
   and E. The "today's" figures in D4, D5, D3, terrain-review and world_plan
   still carry the medium class.
3. **Plans written before F1/F3** (the `procgena_mesh` and JSON era) never got
   a superseded banner. tree/port_plan.md is the only one that did.
4. **Status written into several places.** The same fact (cluster cards,
   fruit impostors, R2 impostors, ScatterInput adoption) is restated in
   ARCHITECTURE's level text, its TODOs, ROADMAP's milestone and the plan, and
   only some copies get updated. A rule that status lives in ROADMAP, with
   other docs linking to it, would stop most of this.
5. **ID namespaces collide:** W1–W6, fruit B1/D1/D2, local D/F/P numbers,
   "GM6", and PB1–4.
